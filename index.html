Dylan Souza

Drummer • Pianist • Guitarist • Composer — California

My name is Dylan Souza and I make music — composing, recording, and releasing songs that blend orchestral textures with rock and progressive elements. I released an EP titled "New World" and play drums in the band Bloody Luxury.

Artists that inspire me include Brandon Burkhalter, Polyphia, CHON, Arch Echo, Scott LePage, and Tim Henson.
